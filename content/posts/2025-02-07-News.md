## CW6

In that week I presented some insights at the Volkswagen Stammtisch, an internal developer weekly meeting to share knowledge. Super cool idea frm Volkswagen to have something like that in the company and I was honored to do so. The content of my slides is already shared in that blog, so I don't have anything to add.

Other topics I did in that week were business usual, except one thing which is really worth mentioning, the learnings from the Quarkus Atomic Cafe.

### Quarkus News (from the Quarkus Atomic Cafe) 

The two most hot topics at the moment are [Model Conztext Protocol](https://quarkus.io/blog/introducing-mcp-servers/) and Agentic AI. 
In the meeting we discussed the content for the next two Quarkus Insight and they were **mind blowing**. Before that meeting I didn't understand both thing at all. After the meeting both are clear for me. 
The code is open and available on github: https://github.com/quarkiverse/quarkus-mcp-servers

[Super recommendation to have a look into the next two Quarkus Insights, here is the link](https://quarkus.io/insights/)

#### Update: 
Both talks are now available. Here are the links:
1. [Episode 195: Agentic AI](https://youtube.com/live/ii-uHYiyxfQ)
2. [Episode 194: Model Context Protocol](https://youtube.com/live/qr7ysMrVxMY)

Other topics were discussed too. Here's a summary:
- granite 3.1 is cool. forget everything before 3.1
- qwen is best for local
- qwen and llama are great for tooling
- deepseek runs faster, but puts train of thoughts into the process and does not have tool support
- other models will learn from deepseek's research
